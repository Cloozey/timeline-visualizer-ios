Don't have your export yet?
  1. On iPhone: Google Maps → profile picture → Settings → Personal content → Export Timeline data
  2. Save the file — it downloads as Timeline.json
  3. Come back here and tap Load Timeline.json, selecting it from Files
Everything happens on your phone. Your file is never uploaded anywhere.
